## Evidence and architecture decision

The current `ClassSession.session_status` is a mixed workflow/finalization field with active consumers and direct writers. `COMPLETED` is not evidence that an institutional session was officially held. The specification therefore preserves current pilot semantics and recommends an additive hybrid architecture:

`OPTION_C_HYBRID_ADDITIVE_EFFECTIVE_OCCURRENCE_PLUS_IMMUTABLE_VERSIONS`

The target occurrence vocabulary is exactly `SCHEDULED, HELD, CANCELLED, RESCHEDULED`, with one occurrence at institutional `ClassSession` grain. Joint sessions remain one institutional occurrence; class-level reporting partitions participants separately. Partial attendance and conflict/review conditions remain workflow/exception data, not new canonical occurrence statuses.

Attendance opportunity filtering is targeted to `OFFICIALLY_HELD` after future occurrence integration. Existing downstream attendance formulas are not rewritten in this task. Legacy `COMPLETED` is not automatically mapped to `HELD`, and no automatic historical backfill is authorized.
