## Service design

`CanonicalSessionOccurrenceService` is the sole intended application-layer owner for canonical occurrence writes. It:

- wraps version creation and effective-pointer movement in one transaction;
- locks the `ClassSession` row with `FOR UPDATE`;
- allocates `version_no` internally from the locked session history;
- uses the effective predecessor for `supersedes_version_id`;
- preserves immutable history;
- ensures participant snapshots before HELD;
- provides `correctEffectiveOccurrence()` as the explicit terminal correction primitive.

Concurrency strategy: `CLASS_SESSION_ROW_LOCK + DB_UNIQUE_CONSTRAINT`.

## Invariants

- initial SCHEDULED, HELD, CANCELLED, and RESCHEDULED observations are supported;
- initial HELD does not synthesize SCHEDULED;
- HELD does not require complete attendance or minimum duration;
- partial HELD requires non-empty `partial_reason`;
- routine terminal overwrite is prohibited;
- routine cancellation/reschedule with attendance is rejected;
- reschedule requires reason and a distinct replacement session;
- correction requires an explicit reason and appends a new version;
- actor is explicit and persisted;
- joint ClassSession produces one occurrence chain;
- legacy `class_sessions.session_status` is never mutated.
