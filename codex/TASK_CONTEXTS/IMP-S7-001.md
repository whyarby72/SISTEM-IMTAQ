# Task Context — IMP-S7-001

## Status
`IN_PROGRESS` — semester subject grade schema/API with provenance.

## Policy

- Canonical grain is one current record per Student × Subject × Semester.
- Score range is 0–100; explicit zero is valid; missing grade is not zero.
- MVP has no Tugas/Quiz/UTS/UAS fields.
- `grade_source` supports `DIRECT_ENTRY` and `IMPORTED`; calculated grades remain future.
- Teaching-assignment provenance and responsible staff are nullable; ambiguous provenance must not be fabricated.
- Exact entry/finalization/lock authority remains `POLICY_PENDING`.

## Boundary

Add the canonical table/model and a minimal internal persistence service that records DRAFT grades with actor/version/audit data. Do not expose official grade-entry authority, finalization, report or transcript behavior.
