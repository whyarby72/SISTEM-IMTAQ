# TASK CONTEXT — IMP-S0-002

## Outcome

Document the application code/module ownership and safe-change conventions needed before business-domain implementation begins.

## Scope

This task establishes written conventions only. It must preserve the existing Laravel foundation and architecture contracts.

### Allowed now

- document change classification, impact statement, file-scope declaration and protected-zone escalation;

### Not allowed now

- Student, Staff, Guardian, Organization or Academic business schema;

- business routes, controllers, models or domain services;
- framework/library upgrade;
- staging/production operation or provider integration.
